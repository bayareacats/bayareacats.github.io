Adoptions

Adoption process

We're open Saturdays from 10 AM to 2 PM at Petco Sunnyvale – just drop by! To visit at another time, please submit an application or email adopt@bayareacats.org. We believe in a simple, straightforward adoption process and allow same-day adoptions.


Adoption application

Available cats & kittens

Visit us at Bay Area Cats' Adoption Center inside Petco at 160 E El Camino Real, Sunnyvale 94087. All cats and kittens are spayed or neutered, vaccinated, microchipped, flea treated, and dewormed.


Place a hold

See a cat you really want?

Place a hold to reserve them and get first priority to adopt. Holds last 24 hours or expire Saturday at 10 AM, whichever is sooner. The hold fee does not apply toward the adoption fee.

To place a hold, submit an application and email adopt@bayareacats.org. We'll contact you with next steps.
