Who we fix

Males can come too when they're booked with a female. A few ways to do that:

  • Bring along a neighbor with a female cat
  • Trap the mom cat that had the kittens
  • Team up with a friend or family member who has a female pet

Pet cats

  • Female pet cats anywhere in Santa Clara County.
  • Males when booked together with a female.
  • Mom's Last Litter (any city): surrender friendly, pet kittens through us and we'll spay mom and every cat in the home, with vaccines and a microchip.
  • Pregnant cats: pet, stray, or trapped, anywhere in the Bay Area.

Stray & community cats

  • Friendly, handleable stray cats and kittens
  • Pregnant feral or stray cats that have already been trapped
  • Additional hands-on support in Sunnyvale, Cupertino, and ZIP code 95127
  • Occasional TNR clinics in other areas when donations allow

Questions

How much does it cost?

Free if you need it. If you can, we ask for a donation to cover the surgery. We're 100% volunteer-run and every surgery is paid for by donations, so we can't keep it free for everyone.

Can I get help if I'm low-income or unhoused?

Yes. We help pet owners regardless of income or housing, including low-income and unhoused families. Tell us your situation on the form.

How do I book a spay/neuter appointment?

Fill out our help form at bayareacats.org/help. Our team contacts you with available dates and books the surgery for you. You don't need to call a vet.

Where do I drop off my cat?

Drop off and pick up are at Petco Sunnyvale, 160 E El Camino Real, Sunnyvale, CA 94087. We confirm times with you when we book.

Do you fix male cats?

We're currently booking female cats. Males can come too when they're booked with a female: bring along a neighbor with a female cat, trap the mom cat, or team up with a friend or family member who has a female pet.

Is there a limit on how many cats I can bring?

No. Whether you have 1 cat or 50, we'll help.

Do you help with stray, feral, or community cats?

Yes. We help with friendly, handleable stray cats and kittens, and pregnant feral or stray cats that have already been trapped. We offer additional hands-on support in Sunnyvale, Cupertino, and ZIP code 95127, and run occasional TNR clinics in other areas when donations allow.

What areas do you serve?

Female pet cats anywhere in Santa Clara County. Our Mom's Last Litter program is open to any city: surrender friendly, pet kittens through us and we'll spay mom and every cat in the home.
