How to get your cat fixed

  1. 1

    Submit our help form

    Fill out the form at bayareacats.org/help. It takes a few minutes.

  2. 2

    We contact you with dates

    Our team reaches out with available dates and books the surgery for you.

  3. 3

    Drop off & pick up at Petco Sunnyvale

    Bring your cat to 160 E El Camino Real, Sunnyvale. We'll confirm drop-off and pick-up times with you.

Who we fix

We're currently booking female cats.

Males can come too when they're booked with a female. A few ways to do that:

  • Bring along a neighbor with a female cat
  • Trap the mom cat that had the kittens
  • Team up with a friend or family member who has a female pet

Pet cats

  • Female pet cats anywhere in Santa Clara County.
  • Males when booked together with a female.
  • Mom's Last Litter (any city): surrender friendly, pet kittens through us and we'll spay mom and every cat in the home, with vaccines and a microchip.
  • Pregnant cats: pet, stray, or trapped, anywhere in the Bay Area.

Stray & community cats

  • Friendly, handleable stray cats and kittens
  • Pregnant feral or stray cats that have already been trapped
  • Additional hands-on support in Sunnyvale, Cupertino, and ZIP code 95127
  • Occasional TNR clinics in other areas when donations allow

How this is paid for

Run by volunteers. Funded by donations.

Bay Area Cats is 100% volunteer-run, and every surgery we book is paid for by donations. We keep it free for anyone who needs it, but we can't sustain free for everyone. If you're able, please donate to cover your cat's surgery or help fund a TNR clinic for a whole colony.
